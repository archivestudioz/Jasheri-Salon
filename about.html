Our Story

About Jasheri

A house built on craft, character, and the belief that beauty is deeply personal.

The House

More than a salon —
a place to belong.

Jasheri House of Beauty was founded with one purpose: to offer the women and men of Hasbrouck Heights an experience as considered as the one they'd find in Manhattan — without leaving the neighborhood.

Every detail of the space has been intentional, from the warm tones of the interior to the curated product shelves. We're not in the business of rushing appointments; we're in the business of craftsmanship.

What We Stand For

Our Philosophy

I

Craft Over Speed

We give every service the time it deserves. No rushed chairs, no shortcuts — just thoughtful, expert work.

II

Personal, Not Formulaic

Your hair, your skin, your lifestyle — every appointment starts with understanding you before we begin.

III

Premium Products

We use only professional-grade products from brands we trust, chosen for performance and integrity.

IV

An Elevated Space

A quiet, considered environment — designed for you to exhale the moment you walk in.

V

Continued Education

Our team trains regularly with industry leaders to stay at the forefront of technique and trend.

VI

Community First

We're proud to call Hasbrouck Heights home, and we treat every guest like part of the house.

The Experience

Every visit,
a ritual.

Arrive to a greeting and a signature beverage. Settle into a consultation where we listen first, recommend second. Every service closes with a styled finish and a take-home routine built for your hair, not anyone else's.

It's a small shift that changes everything — and it's why our guests keep coming back.
